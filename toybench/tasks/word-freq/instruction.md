# Most frequent words

Print the five most frequent words in `speech.txt`.

- A word is a maximal run of ASCII letters (`A` to `Z`, `a` to `z`); every other character separates words. Words are compared case-insensitively.
- Print one line per word, most frequent first, in the form `<word> <count>`: the word in lowercase, a single space, and its number of occurrences. Break ties in count by alphabetical order of the word.
- Print exactly these five lines and nothing else.
